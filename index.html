Definition

What is a Career Journey?

Career Journeys
@ NYUAD

Captions are available in the video player. Can't see the video? Watch the video on Synthesia → (opens in new tab)

Career Journeys is designed as a dedicated space for employees to explore their aspirations, growth, and future opportunities — separate from performance evaluation.

An employee-centered framework

A Career Journey is designed to empower individuals to actively shape their growth, development, and identify future opportunities. Career Journeys put employees in the driver's seat — while the organization provides the tools and support.

Define. Build. Navigate.

It enables employees to define their aspirations, build capabilities, and navigate meaningful experiences across NYU's global ecosystem through clear structures, tools, and organizational partnership.

What makes it different?

Career Journeys are employee-driven, focused on personal growth and readiness — distinct from organizational programmes such as workforce planning or succession planning, which are designed to meet institutional needs.

How development actually happens — the 70/20/10 model

70%
Experiences

On-the-job stretch assignments, new projects, cross-functional work, and taking on broader responsibilities.

20%
Relationships

Learning through feedback, mentoring, coaching conversations with your manager, and peer collaboration.

10%
Training

Formal learning — courses, certifications, workshops, and programmes like the LinkedIn Learning Career Hub.
